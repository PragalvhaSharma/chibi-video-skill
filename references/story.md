# Storyline and pacing

## Fit the story to the song

`analyze_song.py` prints the grid and a loudness map with the song's shape marked:

- **Quiet intro:** the setup (at home, getting ready, bored). Keep it under about 30% of the length.
- **First big lift:** the plan kicks off (the car launches, the door kicks open). Land a flash cut on the exact beat.
- **Middle:** set pieces. One location is 3–6 shots: arrive, build up, payoff, reaction.
- **Dip or breakdown:** the turn. Fade to white, the drive home, a quiet moment.
- **Final chorus:** the ending. Group moments, credits with everyone's names, a big sky, the title, fade to black on the last note.

## Shot rules

- 3–8 beats per shot. Only establishing shots, the credits and the ending go past 8.
- Every shot has at least one event on a beat (a pop, swap, jump or cut), plus constant motion: beat bounces, head bobs and a slow camera push.
- Give everyone a moment and a role, such as driver, winner, loser, the one who eats too much, or the one who falls asleep.
- Running gags pay off twice. Glasses fly off in act 1 and again in the car; the jackpot winner loses the cash at the end.
- Reaction heads in the corners, big text pops ("JACKPOT!!", "K.O.", "DECLINED") and intro cards ("THE DRIVER") carry the jokes. Keep text short and never use lyrics.
- Use the user's real details: street names, restaurants, nicknames, a group-chat name. Ask for inside jokes if there aren't any yet.

## STORYLINE.md format

Acts as sections, each with a table of Time | Beats | Shot | What happens. Put open decisions in [brackets] and list them at the end.

## Occasion shapes

- **Night out:** home, the drive, venue 1, venue 2, food, the drive home, lookout or rooftop ending.
- **Trip:** packing, travel, three places, the last night, going home, photo recap.
- **Birthday:** secret planning, surprise, party gags, cake disaster, wishes montage, group photo.
- **Game day or homecoming:** morning prep in team colours, the street or pregame, the game (close call, then the win), the night, walking home at sunrise.
