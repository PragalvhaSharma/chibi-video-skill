# Shooting Star — JST (worked example)

89.2 s song at 188 BPM (period 0.31915 s, first beat 0.983 s). 46 shots, mostly 4–6 beats each.
Scene ids: a = home, b = the night out, c = the lookout ending. The song's energy dips at about 62–64 s,
which is where the fade to white happens; the final chorus carries the ending.
